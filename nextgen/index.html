What we do

The right gear for every tree.

From a single dead limb over the roof to a full wood lot, NextGen brings the crane, the climber and the grinder, then rakes the yard clean.

01

Tree Removal

Dead, dying or diseased trees taken down safely, even a few inches from a fence or house. Crane picks keep heavy wood off your lawn.

02

Pruning & Trimming

Deadwood, tight unions and crossing branches removed, crowns thinned, and limbs trimmed back from buildings and overhead wires.

03

Stump Grinding

Carbide-tooth grinding below grade, leaving the area ready for topsoil and new sod or grass seed.

04

Line & Township Clearing

Trees cleared from overhead lines, plus township and city tree work. NextGen coordinates with Hydro One when needed.

05

Lot & Wood Lot Clearing

Lots, fence lines and wood lots cleared with a grapple truck that loads logs and brush without wrecking the ground.

06

Cleanup & Haul Away

Every job finishes with brush hauled, the area raked and the yard left clean. Customers say there was nothing left to do.

Meet the owner

Meet James, and the next generation.

NextGen Tree Inc. is a family-owned business founded in 2023 in Woodstock. The goal is simple: help customers and the community look after their trees, so the next generation inherits healthy trees in our neighbourhoods.

In just a few years James and his crew have earned a perfect 5.0 on Google from 41 reviews, for careful removals in tight spots, quick communication and cleanup so thorough customers say there's nothing left to do.
