FAQ

Tree removal questions

Do you do emergency tree removal?

Yes. Tree King is open 24 hours for storm damage and dangerous trees. Call 613-627-2017.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Ottawa?

Often, yes. In Ottawa's urban area a private tree 30 cm or more across at 1.3 m, on a lot of one hectare or less, usually needs a City permit with a Tree Information Report. We tell you up front and prepare the report.

Is the quote free?

Yes. We walk the property with you and give you a detailed written quote with a clear scope, whether a permit is likely, and whether stump grinding is included.

Can you remove a tree right next to my house?

Yes. Large, brittle or boxed-in trees come down with a crane taking the weight, so the climber can make clean cuts and every piece is lowered clear of the house.

Is stump grinding included?

Only if you ask for it. Stump grinding can be quoted with the removal or booked as a follow-up. Stumps are ground 6 to 8 inches below grade, ready for grass.

Are you insured?

Yes. We are insured for tree work, and proof of insurance is available on request.

Do you top trees?

No. Topping leaves stubs, weak sprouts and wounds that are hard to close. We will say when a tree is worth keeping.
