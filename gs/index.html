Meet the owner

Meet Paul, your local arborist.

Paul Goldstone grew up spending his summers at his grandparents' cabin in Riding Mountain's Old Campground. Today he's an ISA Certified Arborist with more than ten years of experience, and has left his mark across Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Alberta and British Columbia.

He knows the unique needs of the communities around Harrison Park, because they're his neighbours.
