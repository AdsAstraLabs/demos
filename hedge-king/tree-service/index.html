Tree service & maintenance in Ottawa

Hedge King's arborists look after the trees as well as the hedges: tree removal, trimming and stump grinding.

Call 613-265-0908Free Quote

Tree removal

Our skilled team removes trees of all shapes and sizes. The most common method is climbing the tree and limbing branches on the way up, topping it near the crown, then taking the trunk down in sections on the way back down.

Tree trimming

Trimming keeps a tree and your landscape healthy, whether it is low branches over the driveway or walkway, or limbs higher up rubbing on the house.

Stump grinding

A stump left behind is an eyesore and can be a tripping hazard. We grind it out below grade so grass or flowers can be planted instead.

Bigger tree jobs

For crane-assisted removals and City of Ottawa permit reports, our sister company Tree King Ottawa has you covered.
