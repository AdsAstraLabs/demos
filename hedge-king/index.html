FAQ
How often should my hedge be trimmed?

Cedar hedges should be trimmed professionally once a year, around the same time each year. That keeps the hedge full and rich in colour.

Is Hedge King insured?

Yes. Hedge King is fully insured with a claim-free history, and a copy of our insurance is available to review at any time.

When should I get my hedge trimmed?

When there is no frost or risk of frost. In Ottawa that is typically any time from late April to late October.

What happens to the trimmings?

After a thorough clean-up we haul the trimmings away. If the trailer fills up on a big job, we stack the rest neatly at the end of the driveway and our friends at Recycled Cedars pick them up.

Can an overgrown hedge be cut back?

Somewhat. About a quarter of the height can come off the top, and often only around an eighth of the width. Beyond that, replacement is usually the better option.

Do you do tree work too?

Yes. Our arborists handle tree trimming, tree removal and stump grinding.
