Services

Southwood Tree Service provides a wide range of private and commercial tree related services.

  • 01

    Tree removal

    We have the expertise and equipment to address both small and large sized tree removal projects.

  • 02

    Pruning

    Need help with trimming and pruning your existing trees, pruning away from your house, deadwooding? Let us help.

  • 03

    Tree climbing

    Do you have a tree on your property you need removed or pruned where equipment can’t access it? We are professional climbing arborists.

  • 04

    Stump grinding

    Safely and efficiently remove unsightly tree stumps from your yard or property.

  • 05

    Cabling & bracing

    Have a tree that needs saving? Reinforcement can safely improve or restore a tree’s structural integrity.

  • 06

    Planting

    Planting trees and shrubs can make a world of difference in your yard. We are here to help.

  • 07

    Tree structure analysis

    We can perform tree structural analysis to determine current and potential risks to you and your property.

  • 08

    Tree emergencies

    If you or your property is threatened, damaged or obstructed by fallen trees or branches, we are here to help 24/7.

Animal rescue

We offer cat and animal rescue from trees. They don’t always come down on their own. If an animal has been in a tree for longer than 24 hours, give us a call.
