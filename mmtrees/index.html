Meet the owner

Meet Mirek, The Brant Tree People.

M & M Tree Service has served Brantford and Brant County for over 25 years. Owner Mirek Turczyniak still climbs the jobs himself, and neighbours notice. One Homestars reviewer wrote that he “reminds me of a squirrel when he safely climbs.”

The mission is simple: safety, education, teamwork and the environment. Every emailed estimate includes a copy of liability insurance and the Master Business License.
