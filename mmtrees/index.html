About M & M

Meet Mirek, owner and climber.

M & M Tree Service is owned by Mirek Turczyniak, an ISA Certified Arborist who has been caring for Brantford and Brant County trees for over 25 years. The team pairs careful removals and pruning with a Wood Shop that turns taken-down trees into firewood, lumber and mulch. A copy of liability insurance and Master Business License goes out with every emailed estimate.
