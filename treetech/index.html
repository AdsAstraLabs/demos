Meet Everest

A personal mission, climbed one tree at a time

TREE TECH is my personal mission to serve Northwestern Ontario with the very best and safest practices in Arboriculture.Everest Rymer, owner and arborist

Everest started with plants long before trees. Today he climbs, rigs and plans every job himself, catering to people who care about their trees, the natural landscape, and having the work done safely and efficiently.

Good to know

Is the tree assessment really free?

Yes. For life in the trees, the assessment is free.

Do you work at cottages and on islands?

Yes. Much of the work is at lake houses, cottages and island properties on Lake of the Woods and around Kenora.

Can you take down a tree next to my cabin?

That's the specialty. Rope access and heavy technical rigging let big trees come down in pieces, exactly where they should, over decks, roofs and water.

What happens to the wood?

Along with the free firewood program, there's a chainsaw milling service if you'd like your trees turned into mantles, table tops or benches.

Do you handle emergencies?

Yes. If your property is damaged or in danger from a tree, you get priority service, any hour of the day.
