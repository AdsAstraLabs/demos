Got questions?

How much do you charge?

All quotes are free and there’s no obligation. Every job is unique: pricing depends on the size, location and complexity of the work. We’ll come out, assess the situation and email you a detailed quote. No surprises.

How quickly can you come out?

We pride ourselves on being fast and responsive. Response time depends on our current schedule, but we always prioritize emergency situations. Contact us and we’ll tell you exactly when we can be there.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. Bison Tree Service is fully licensed and carries complete liability insurance. You’re fully protected on every job we perform. We’re happy to provide documentation on request.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Hamilton, Ancaster, Dundas, Stoney Creek, Caledonia & Binbrook, plus the surrounding areas. Not listed? Call us.

What are your business hours?

Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM. We’re closed Sundays. For emergencies, don’t hesitate to call, whatever the time.

What’s the best way to get in touch?

Feel free to call, text or email us. You can reach us at (289) 683-5408 or bisontrees@gmail.com. You can also fill out the quote form and we’ll get back to you quickly.

Do you clean up after the job?

Yes, always. We leave your property clean and tidy. All debris, branches and wood are removed or chipped on-site. Your yard will look great when we’re done.
