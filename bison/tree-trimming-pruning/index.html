How often should my trees be trimmed?

Most mature trees every 3 to 5 years. Young and fruit trees, more often.

When is the best time to trim in Ontario?

Late winter suits most. Maples and birches bleed sap then, so we do them mid-summer.

Will trimming harm my tree?

Proper trimming helps. Topping does lasting damage, so we never do it.

Do you clean up after?

Yes. Chipped on site and hauled away.
