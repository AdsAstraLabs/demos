Tips

  • Keep together stops words from being split across two lines (for example a name or “10:30 am”). It wraps the selected words in <span class="nw">…</span>.
  • Fixed space inserts &nbsp;, a space that never breaks a line.
  • Times are kept together with “am”, “pm” and “h” automatically, and a short last word is not left alone on a line.
  • Photos: choose one from the library or upload a new one (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 15 MB). Always describe what the photo shows, for people using screen readers.
