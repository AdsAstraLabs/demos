Why chip instead of haul

Chipping is the common way to get rid of limbs and branches once they are cut, and it is an essential part of any tree removal: cutting a tree down always means dealing with the material. Once chipped, the wood can be hauled away, used as bark mulch, or spread back over the land to feed the soil, which makes chipping an environmentally friendly way to recycle tree waste.

Our chippers

Bisson Services runs a range of well-maintained wood chippers, from 10" to 20", brought right to your door so most tree material can be processed on site. Species such as Cedar and Pine make superior garden mulch.

Whether it follows an emergency removal, a regular removal or a landscaping project, the chips go directly into our mulch trucks to keep things neat. The mulch can be left on site, or we are happy to take it away. The choice is yours.

Benefits of wood chipping

  • Reduces a large amount of yard debris without burning
  • Wood chips can be used for growing mushrooms
  • Wood chips can line paths or mulch garden beds
  • Tidies the yard in line with your municipality's rules
