What we do

Six core services, done the right way.

Tree Cutting & Removal

Crane-assisted and bucket-truck removals of dead, dying and dangerous trees, even large trees in difficult positions.

24-Hour Emergency

Storms move fast, so do we. One call for dead, dying or dangerous trees, day or night.

Stump Grinding

Stump grinding after your tree removal, by the same crew.

Wood Chipping

Brush and limbs chipped on site.

Tree Pruning

Pruning that improves airflow, structure and the long-term health of trees, from low limbs to the tops of mature trees.

Hedge & Shrub Trimming

Cedar hedges and shrubs kept dense, healthy and even, trimmed twice a year, with every clipping cleaned up.
