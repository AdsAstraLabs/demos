Is a tree in the wrong place?

Bisson Services can help you decide whether a tree should be cut or removed. Trees too close to houses and other structures, or species planted in the wrong location, are candidates for removal. They are a threat in strong thunderstorms and under ice and snow, and if one loses branches or falls it is likely to cause costly damage.

Trees in the wrong spot can also damage foundations, lift sidewalks, cause mildew on siding, rub paint off structures, clog gutters with leaves, and give squirrels, ants and other critters a path into your home or business.

When we recommend removing a tree

  • The tree is dead or dying
  • The tree is harming other trees
  • The tree is considered dangerous
  • The tree causes an obstruction that pruning and trimming cannot correct

Cut down safely, by insured professionals

Need a tree cut down and hauled away, or not sure whether one is dead? Tree cutting is dangerous work and should be done by experienced, insured tree cutters. To make sure no damage is done to your property, let our experienced arborists handle the cutting and removal.

Whether it is a simple cut or a complex, multi-day removal, a Bisson Services professional will meet you on site to decide which trees need to come down and to plan the best way to do it. The estimate is free.

Big & tight removals

For mature trees near homes, roads and lines, Bisson works the crane, telehandler and bucket truck in tandem, taking the tree down in controlled sections and lowering each piece clear of the house.

Tree Removal: common questions

Do you offer emergency tree removal?

Yes. For dead, dying and dangerous trees, Bisson Services provides 24-hour emergency service. Call (613) 558-4806.

When should a tree be removed instead of pruned?

When it is dead or dying, harming other trees, considered dangerous, or causing an obstruction that pruning cannot correct.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. A Bisson Services professional visits the property, decides with you which trees need to come down, and plans the job.
