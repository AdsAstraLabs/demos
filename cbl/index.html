FAQs

Got questions?

Don’t see yours? Call 705-931-2227 and ask Joe.

Is your team insured?

Yes. We’re fully insured and registered with WSIB, and safety is our top priority.

How can I request a quote?

Fill out our form for a free, no-obligation quote. Owner and ISA Certified Arborist Joe Corkery assesses your trees in person.

How much does tree removal cost?

It depends on the tree’s size, location and condition. Quotes are free, in person and no obligation.

Do I need a permit to remove my tree?

In the City of Peterborough, yes, for trees 7.5 cm or wider in diameter. We handle the whole permit process.

Can you remove trees near buildings or fences?

Yes. We specialize in challenging removals, using rigging or cranes to minimize damage.

What happens to the wood and branches?

We chip and haul away all debris, unless you’d like to keep the wood for firewood or mulch.

How quickly can you come out to remove a fallen tree?

We prioritize emergency calls and we’re available 24/7.

Can my tree be saved, or does it need to be removed?

Not always removal. Pruning or deadwood removal may be enough. If a tree is severely damaged or unsafe, we’ll say so in your assessment.
