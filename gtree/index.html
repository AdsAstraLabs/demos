Meet the owner

Meet Graeme, home in Grey-Bruce.

Graeme MacIntyre was born and raised in Shallow Lake, just outside Owen Sound. After graduating from Sir Sandford Fleming College, he worked as an arborist for a private tree service, then spent 15 years as a valued member of a municipal forestry crew in Guelph, where G Tree Care was also established.

In June 2020 Graeme, his wife Dana and their family moved home, and G Tree Care came with them. Today he serves Owen Sound, Grey and Bruce counties with the same professionalism, reliability and quality that made him widely referred in Guelph.
