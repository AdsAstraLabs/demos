What we do

Tree care, start to finish.

From one overgrown hedge to a row of tall trees beside the house, Kyle's crew climbs, rigs, grinds and cleans up so you don't have to.

01

Tree Removal

Dead, hazardous or unwanted trees taken down in sections, including tight spots near homes, sheds and hydro lines.

02

Pruning & Trimming

Deadwood, crossing limbs and overextended branches removed for a safer, healthier, better-looking tree.

03

Stump Grinding

Stumps ground below grade and the area raked clean, ready for lawn, garden or a new tree.

04

Hedge & Shrub Care

Crisp, shaped hedges and shrubs, from small front yards to long property lines.

05

Planting & Deep Root Fertilization

The right tree in the right spot, plus deep root feeding to keep established trees thriving.

06

Chipping & Cleanup

Branches chipped on site and debris hauled away. Ask about free wood chip drop-offs.

Meet the owner

Meet Kyle, hooked since his first climb.

Godon Tree Care Services Ltd. was founded and is owned by Kyle Godon, an Ontario Qualified Arborist who graduated from Humber College's Arboriculture program with Honours.

Kyle has climbed, removed, pruned and ground stumps all over the GTA, from Toronto and Scarborough to Burlington and Schomberg. Today his fully insured, family-owned team works on residential and urban properties of every shape and size, with the safety gear to match.
