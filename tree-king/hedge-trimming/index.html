Why hire a pro for hedges

Getting picture-perfect hedges is easy to mess up. Improper trimming or shearing can take off too much foliage, leave them uneven, or cause long-term damage that is expensive or impossible to fix. Different hedges need different trimming schedules, and our team knows which regimen fits yours.

Even the tall ones

Hedge King reshaped a massive, overgrown 30 ft cedar hedge in Ottawa using a 70 ft spider lift. If yours has got away from you, we have the reach.
