FAQ

Questions we get asked

A tree is on the power line. What do I do?

Stay well back, keep everyone away, and call your electricity distributor and 911 first. Once the line is made safe, we deal with the tree.

Do you really answer the phone at night?

Yes. Emergency calls are answered day and night. If there are more urgent situations ahead of you, we will tell you honestly when we can get there.

A big limb is caught up in my tree. Can it wait?

No. A hung limb can sit for weeks and then fall on a calm day. It is a small job now and a serious incident later.

Should I cut it up myself while I wait?

Please do not. Storm-damaged wood is under tension you cannot see and it moves hard when cut. Keep people and pets away and call us.
